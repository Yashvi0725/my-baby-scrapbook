a note for you ✎

To my favourite person,

When I look at this photo, I see one of those little moments I wish I could keep in a tiny glass jar forever. Your hand on my face, that smile, and the way being beside you feels so natural.

Somewhere along the way, you became the person I want to tell everything to — the silly things, the good news, the random thoughts, and even the things I don't know how to explain yet.

I don't need every moment with you to be perfect. I just want more of these real, unplanned moments where we can be ourselves. I'm so glad our paths crossed.

With a heart full of warmth, ♡

your person, always
