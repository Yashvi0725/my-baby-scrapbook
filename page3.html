a note for you ✎

A little secret,

This night, these lights, our outfits, and us sitting close together — it may look like one photo, but to me it holds a whole evening of little details I want to remember.

I want to remember the laughter between pictures, the conversations no one else heard, the little looks, and the comfortable silence when we didn't need to say much at all.

Maybe that's what makes a memory precious: not that everything was perfect, but that it was ours. I hope we keep collecting nights like this, one tiny story at a time.

You make ordinary memories feel golden. ♡
