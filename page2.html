PAGE 02 · THE FEELING

You make my heart
forget to be shy.

a note for you ✎

Hey, you,

This picture says so much without needing a single word. You leaning in close, the sky above us, and that tiny moment that somehow feels bigger in my memory than it probably looked from the outside.

You make affection feel easy and happiness feel close. Sometimes I catch myself smiling at a memory of you, and I don't even try to hide it.

I hope you know you never have to perform or be perfect around me. I like the real you — the laughing you, the quiet you, the goofy you, and every version in between.

If this feeling had a colour, it'd be you. ♡

the one who adores you
